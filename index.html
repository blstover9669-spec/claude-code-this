Arm-powered tag on floating mushroom islands. No legs — just your hands.

How to play

In VR: your hands are solid. Push against the ground, walls or mushrooms to move your body, and let go mid-swing to fling yourself. Swing both arms back along the ground to run; slam down and release to jump; pull down on trunks to climb.

  • Pink spotted caps are bouncy — slap them to launch.
  • Crystals are slippery. Clouds are soft and climbable.
  • Punch the buttons on the wooden console to pick a mode, bots and difficulty.
  • Right thumbstick: snap turn (toggle it on the console).

Modes: Tag · Spore Tag (infection) · Orb Rush · Lava Rise.

Desktop: WASD move · mouse look · Space jump · Shift sprint · hold W against a wall to climb · click to slap / press buttons · Enter start/stop · Q/E change mode · M menu.
